#### THE PURPOSE BEHIND COMANDA

Comanda is a platform that enables establishments to manage orders and customer consumption through a unified digital experience.

The platform supports three ordering modalities: online ordering through the establishment's digital catalog, in-person counter ordering, and in-person tab-based ordering. All modalities operate within the same centralized workflow, allowing businesses to manage products, orders, payments and customer interactions from a single system.

Comanda aims to simplify the ordering and payment experience while reducing the operational complexity associated with manual or fragmented processes.

By providing a centralized and digital workflow, Comanda helps establishments process orders more efficiently, maintain visibility over customer consumption and streamline day-to-day operations.

#### THE TARGET AUDIENCE

Comanda is primarily designed for small and medium-sized food service establishments, with an initial focus on fast-food restaurants and similar businesses that require a simple and efficient way to manage orders, customer consumption and payments.

The platform is built to address the operational needs commonly found in growing establishments, where manual processes, paper-based workflows or disconnected systems can negatively impact service quality and operational efficiency.

While the current focus is on the fast-food segment, the platform is designed with future expansion in mind, allowing support for additional business models and service environments as the product evolves.

#### THE VALUE PROPOSITION

Comanda is designed to provide establishments with a simple and predictable operating model.

Instead of relying on transaction-based pricing, the platform focuses on delivering operational value through a fixed subscription model, allowing businesses to grow without increasing platform costs as sales volume increases.

By centralizing operations into a single platform, establishments can reduce manual processes, improve visibility and maintain direct ownership of their customer relationships.
